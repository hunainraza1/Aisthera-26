The Story

What is Aisthera?

Inspired by the Greek aisthēsis — the sensation, perception, and experience of the world — Aisthera is a place where curiosity opens hidden doors and imagination takes unexpected turns. From spirited competitions and clever challenges to vibrant performances and creative adventures, there's something waiting for every kind of mind.

Whether you're here to compete for the win, cheer from the sidelines, discover a hidden talent, or simply make memories with friends — Aisthera welcomes you in.

The Rundown

  1. Report by 8:00 a.m. — Check in at the registration desk with your college ID and permission letter.
  2. Pick your events — Compete in on-stage showcases, off-stage creative challenges, or both. Registration per event happens through this site beforehand.
  3. Arrive 30 minutes early — Report to your event's venue at least 30 minutes before its scheduled time.
  4. Compete & create — Follow event-specific rules (below) and the instructions of coordinators and volunteers.
  5. Meals included — Breakfast and lunch are provided to all registered participants by the host college.
  6. Overall Championship — Each college's combined performance across all events counts toward the Overall Championship title.

Archetypes, brought to life on the runway

From the fearless Hero to the untamed Rebel, the wise Sage to the commanding Ruler, the passionate Lover to the unpredictable Trickster. Choose the persona that calls to you and let your fashion reveal the psychology behind the character.

Fine Print

General Rules

Applicable to all events:

  1. All participants must carry and present their valid college ID cards throughout the fest.
  2. Participants are required to report to the registration desk by 8:00 a.m. on the day of the event.
  3. A permission letter from the respective college must be submitted at the time of registration.
  4. Breakfast and lunch will be provided to all registered participants by the host college.
  5. Online registrations will close on 10 October 2026.
  6. If the maximum participant capacity for any competition is reached, the organising committee reserves the right to close registrations for that event at any time, without prior notice.
  7. Participants must report to the designated venue at least 30 minutes before the scheduled event time.
  8. All participants must follow the instructions of the event coordinators and volunteers.
  9. Any form of plagiarism, use of uncredited content, or violation of originality requirements may result in disqualification.
  10. Content must comply with the ethical guidelines and standards of the hosting institution.
  11. Any vulgar, offensive, discriminatory, defamatory, or otherwise inappropriate content may result in disqualification.
  12. Participants are responsible for submitting all required audio, video, photographs, or other materials within the specified deadlines.
  13. The organisers reserve the right to modify event schedules or technical requirements when necessary.
  14. The decision of the judging panel and organising committee will be final and binding.

Overall Championship

  • Awarded to the college that demonstrates the best overall performance across all competitions.
  • Each participating college may register a maximum of 25 participants for the entire fest.
  • Those 25 participants must collectively cover all competitions — no more than 25 may represent a single college under any circumstances.
  • Open to both Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students.
